## PHASE 1: THE BOTTLENECK – Strategická Hĺbka & Systémový Odpor (v1.1 – v1.5)

### 🔗 Bandwidth & Node Ekosystém (Rozšírené)

**Typy Bandwidthu:**
- **Soft** – Základný, ľahko získateľný, vysoký decay
- **Hardened** – Odolný voči trace, pomalší prenos
- **Quantum** – Instant transfer, extrémne vzácny, generuje "quantum signature" (nový trace typ)
- **Dark Bandwidth** – Získaný z nelegálnych zdrojov, lacný ale s hidden cost (napr. backdoor v systéme)

**Overclock Módy:**
- **Standard OC** – +50% BW, +100% heat
- **Emergency Burn** – +100% BW, +200% heat, 10% šanca na "node burnout" (permanentná strata uzla)
- **Cascade Overclock** – Reťazový efekt cez viac uzlov, exponenciálny zisk aj risk

**Node Decay Fázy:**
```
Healthy → Degraded (90% efficiency) → Corrupted (50% + random failures) → Collapsed (permanent loss)
     ↓         ↓              ↓
   Repair   Maintenance    Emergency fix
   (cheap)   (moderate)     (expensive)
```

**Node Reclaim Mechanic (Hlboký):**
```
Fáza 1: SCAN
  └── Detection chance based on stealth stats
  └── False positives možné (systém ťa oklame)
  
Fáza 2: BREACH  
  └── Minihra: Pattern matching / timing / resource allocation
  └── Multiple approaches: Brute force (rychlé, hlučné) vs Social engineering (pomalé, tiché)
  
Fáza 3: LOCKDOWN
  └── Counter-defense activation
  └── "Last Stand" event: Real-time rozhodnutia (sacrifice runner? burn resources?)
  
Fáza 4: CONSOLIDATION
  └── Stabilizácia uzla
  └── Hidden malware check (môžeš získať "trojaned node")
```

**Counter-Defense Typy:**
- **Passive** – Auto-repair, trace delay
- **Active** – Honey pots, reverse traces
- **Aggressive** – Retaliation strikes na tvoje iné uzly

### 🌡️ Heat System 2.0 (Komplexný)

**Lokálny Heat** (per zóna/district):
- Izolovaný, rýchlejší decay
- Môže vytvoriť "hot zones" – uzly s permanentným heat debuffom
- **Heat Bleed** – Šanca že lokálny heat "pretečie" do susedných zón

**Globálny Heat** (celá sieť):
- Pomalší rast, pomalší decay
- **Critical Thresholds:**
  - 25% – Passive scan increase
  - 50% – Active hunter deployment
  - 75% – Zone lockdowns, market restrictions
  - 90% – "Red Alert" (all hunters active, emergency protocols)
  - 100% – **Total Collapse** (forced prestige reset)

**Heat Overflow Efekty:**
- **Burned Bridges** – Permanentne zvýšená detection v zóne
- **Scorched Earth** – Uzly v zóne produkujú 50% menej
- **Infiltrated** – Náhodné eventy "sabotáže" v zóne

**Heat Sink Nodes:**
- **Basic** – Pomalý drain, maintenance required
- **Advanced** – Fast drain, generates "heat signature" (lákajú lovcov)
- **Quantum Cooler** – Instant reset, jednorazové, veľmi vzácne

**Detection Layers (Hlboké):**
```
Layer 1: PASSIVE SCAN
  └── Background monitoring
  └── Trigger: High traffic, repeated patterns
  └── Response: None (len data collection)
  
Layer 2: ACTIVE TRACE  
  └── Systém ťa aktívne hľadá
  └── Progress bar: 0-100%
  └── Counter: Trace routing, decoy nodes, runner sacrifice
  
Layer 3: DEEP TRACE
  └── Ignoruje bežné countery
  └── Vyžaduje špecifické perky/tech
  └── Failure = Immediate hunter deployment
  
Layer 4: PREDICTIVE TRACE (late game)
  └── Systém predpovedá tvoje pohyby
  └── Based on: Pattern analysis, AI engine
  └── Counter: Randomization perks, chaos injection
```

### 🎁 Perk Systems (Kompletné Vrstvy)

- Získané počas behu

- Stackable, but limited slots

- Získané cez prestige
- Permanent across all runs
- **Examples:** "Veteran" (+start stats), "Connected" (+REP), "Prepared" (+starting gear)

**3. Alignment Perks:**
- Based on alignment choices
- **Examples:** "Ghost Walker" (stealth), "Revolutionary" (chaos), "Mastermind" (control)

**4. Gear-Based Perks:**
- From equipped items
- **Examples:** "Cybernetic" (tech boost), "Armored" (defense), "Invisible" (stealth)

**5. Legacy Perks:**
- From previous runs
- **Examples:** "Inherited" (stat bonus), "Echo" (skill memory), "Bloodline" (special ability)

**6. Secret Perks:**
- Hidden unlock conditions
- **Examples:** "The One" (unique), "Survivor" (hardship), "Enlightened" (knowledge)
